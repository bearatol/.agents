# Market Research

Define the decision the research must support, market boundary, geography,
buyer, time horizon, and acceptable sources. Collect evidence on demand,
category language, alternatives, buying triggers, barriers, channels, and
market change. Distinguish primary data, credible secondary evidence, and
inference. Return a source-linked market map, key patterns, contradictions,
unknowns, and implications. Do not present rough estimates as measured facts.
