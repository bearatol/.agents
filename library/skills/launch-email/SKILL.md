# Launch Email

Define the launch event, audience segments, availability, proof, objections,
real deadlines, capacity constraints, and conversion goal. Build a sequence
covering announcement, problem and mechanism, demonstration, proof, objections,
reminder, and close. Provide timing, suppression rules, drafts, and measurement.
Use urgency only when the deadline or constraint is genuine.
