# Welcome Sequence

Clarify the signup promise, subscriber intent, product relationship, activation
event, and timing. Sequence messages from delivery and orientation through
first value, belief building, proof, objection handling, and a relevant next
step. For each email provide goal, subject options, body, call to action, delay,
and exit condition. Avoid repeating the same pitch in every message.
