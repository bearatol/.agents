# Newsletter

Define the reader promise, editorial territory, cadence, recurring sections,
voice, source standards, and business role. Choose one primary idea per issue,
support it with useful detail, and connect promotion to reader value. Return the
issue brief, subject options, complete draft, source notes, and repurposing plan.
Do not pad a weak idea to meet a schedule.
