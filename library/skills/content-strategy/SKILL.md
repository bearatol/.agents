# Content Strategy

Define audience problems, buying stages, business outcomes, distribution,
available expertise, production capacity, and measurement. Choose a small set
of content pillars and formats with a clear role in discovery, trust, activation,
or retention. Return the strategy, topic map, editorial rules, distribution
loop, calendar logic, ownership, and metrics. Avoid calendars filled with
unprioritized ideas.
