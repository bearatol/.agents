# Ad Strategy

Define the audience, objective, funnel stage, offer, channel behavior, tracking,
budget, target economics, exclusions, and creative constraints. Turn assumptions
into testable audience, message, format, and landing-page hypotheses. Return the
campaign structure, allocation, test matrix, naming convention, decision rules,
and risk controls. Do not recommend scaling without conversion and unit-economic
evidence.
