# Positioning

Identify best-fit customers, their urgent situation, real alternatives,
distinct capabilities, resulting value, and the market frame that makes those
advantages understandable. Test each distinction for truth, relevance, and
defensibility. Return a positioning statement, evidence table, unsuitable
segments, and implications for product, sales, and copy. Do not confuse a
slogan with positioning.
