# Copywriting

Confirm audience, awareness, page or channel goal, offer, evidence, objections,
voice, and constraints. Lead with the most relevant outcome or problem, explain
the mechanism, support claims with proof, reduce uncertainty, and make the next
action clear. Produce a strong primary draft plus only meaningfully different
alternatives. Mark any claim that still needs evidence.
