# Copy Editing

Audit the draft against its audience, objective, offer, evidence, and voice.
Fix unclear hierarchy, vague claims, repetition, jargon, unsupported certainty,
awkward rhythm, and weak calls to action. Preserve meaning and verified facts.
Return the edited copy and a short list of unresolved strategic or evidence
issues; do not disguise strategy gaps as wording fixes.
