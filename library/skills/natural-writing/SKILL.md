# Natural Writing

Use for customer-facing prose, articles, emails, documentation, and final
answer cleanup.

## Method

1. Identify audience, purpose, voice, and claims that must remain exact.
2. Replace abstract nouns and bureaucratic phrases with concrete verbs.
3. Remove throat-clearing, repeated conclusions, canned transitions, and
   empty enthusiasm.
4. Vary sentence length naturally and keep paragraphs focused on one idea.
5. Prefer ordinary punctuation. Use dashes only when they improve meaning.
6. Preserve technical terms, legal qualifiers, citations, and deliberate tone.
7. Read the result once for rhythm and once for factual drift.

Return the edited text without explaining every edit unless requested. Do not
add personal anecdotes, false certainty, slang, or artificial imperfections
just to appear human.
