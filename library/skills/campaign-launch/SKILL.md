# Campaign Launch

Confirm objective, audience, offer, message, channels, assets, owners, budget,
dates, tracking, compliance, and decision thresholds. Build a dependency-aware
launch plan with preflight checks for links, analytics, consent, proof, brand,
mobile rendering, landing continuity, and rollback. Return the timeline, owner
matrix, asset checklist, event map, dashboard requirements, and post-launch
review schedule. Block launch when critical evidence or tracking is missing.
