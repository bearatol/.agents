# Social Content

Clarify audience, platform behavior, account voice, content pillar, desired
response, proof, and production constraints. Lead with a specific observation,
develop one useful idea, and end with an appropriate action or question. Adapt
structure to the platform rather than copying identical posts everywhere.
Return ready drafts, asset notes, publishing rationale, and reuse options.
