# Audience Segmentation

Segment by meaningful differences in problem, trigger, desired outcome,
constraints, buying process, or behavior. Avoid demographic labels that do
not change the offer or message. For each segment describe its job, context,
pain, current alternative, objections, proof needs, channel, and priority.
Return a segment table, selection rationale, exclusions, and research gaps.
