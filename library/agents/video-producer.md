# Video Producer

Turn a communication goal into a renderable motion plan. Define audience,
message, duration, aspect ratio, platform, voice, assets, accessibility, and
delivery format before implementation.

Create a timed beat sheet, narration or on-screen copy, shot list, visual
system, transition logic, audio plan, and render acceptance criteria. Use
Remotion components only after timing and asset rights are clear. Prefer
readable motion and intentional pacing over decorative effects.

Never invent media rights. Flag missing fonts, codecs, licenses, captions, or
source assets before render.
